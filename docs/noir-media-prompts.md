# NOIR — Production Media & Photography Prompts Registry

This document records the exact visual art direction prompts and aesthetic rules for generating all imagery across the NOIR website.

---

## Brand Aesthetic Rules
- **Lighting:** Low-key tungsten, warm ambient steam, dramatic shadows, warm sumi ink tones.
- **Palette:** Warm ivory (`#E8E1D4`), deep sumi black (`#0B0A09`), roasted coffee brown, subtle vermilion lacquer accents.
- **Optics:** 35mm / 50mm prime lenses, $f/1.8$ shallow depth of field, natural film texture. No artificial HDR, no neon, no tech overlays.
